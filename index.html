1. About us

At Aurelia Med Spa, we believe great skin is quiet confidence. Our licensed injectors and aestheticians pair medical-grade treatments with a calm, private setting in the heart of Miami, so every visit feels less like an appointment and more like a pause. From your first consultation, we listen, plan with care, and deliver natural results that look like you, only more rested.
